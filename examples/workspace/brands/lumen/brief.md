# Lumen Studio (fictional example brand)

## What we sell
A small yoga and mobility studio in Lisbon. Drop-in classes €15, 10-class pack €120, online mobility program €29/month.

## Ideal customer
Remote workers 25–45 in Lisbon with back and neck pain from laptops. Afraid yoga is "not for them" or too spiritual.

## Markets
Lisbon (EN + PT), online worldwide (EN).

## Offers
- First class free · https://example.com/first-class
- Online mobility · €29/month · https://example.com/online

## Conversion paths
- DM → first-class link · Website → booking page

## Pillars
1. Desk pain fixes (60-second routines)
2. Behind the studio
3. Member stories (with permission)

## Voice
Plain, warm, a little dry. No "namaste", no "journey", no chakras. Talk like a physio friend.

## Claims we can make
- "Most members feel less stiff after two weeks" only with the member survey link.

## Never
- Medical promises ("cures", "fixes your back")
- Before/after body photos

## Visual rules
Natural light, real members, no stock poses. Never generate the logo.

## Competitors
- Big chain gym classes · cheaper, crowded

## Listening keywords
lisbon back pain, desk neck pain, yoga for stiff people, remote work posture

## FAQ
- Q: Do I need a mat? A: No, we have mats.

## Auto-reply allowed
Opening hours, prices, "do I need a mat".

## Regulated topics
Health claims (EU consumer law).
